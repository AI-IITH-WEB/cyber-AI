About IITH

Learn from IIT Hyderabad Faculty and Industry Leaders to Master Cybersecurity and AI Expertise

AI Faculty

Faculty Members

Dr. Karthik P N

Assistant Professor

Dr. Karthik P N is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology, Hyderabad (IIT Hyderabad). Prior to joining IIT Hyderabad, he was a Research Fellow in the Institute of Data Science at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He holds a Ph.D. and MSc Engineering dual degree from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, where he worked in the Department of Electrical Communication Engineering. He also holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electronics and Communications from R V College of Engineering, Bengaluru.

Dr. Konda Reddy Mopuri

Assistant Professor

Dr. Konda Reddy Mopuri is an assistant professor at the Department of Artificial Intelligence and a TiHAN faculty fellow at IIT Hyderabad. Before joining IIT Hyderabad, Dr. Mopuri was a postdoctoral fellow at the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh, UK. He briefly served at IIT Guwahati before moving to Hyderabad. He obtained his Ph.D. in Deep Learning and Computer Vision from IISc Bangalore. His thesis won the IUPRAI Best Doctoral Dissertation Award and the SPCOM Best Doctoral Dissertation Award 2018. He was awarded the Young Alumni Achiever Award for 2022 by his Alma mater, the Dept. of CDS, IISc Bengaluru. His research interests are Artificial Intelligence, Machine learning (specifically Deep Learning), Data Science and Engineering, and Computer Vision. His work has been published in top-tier conferences and journals in AI.

Dr. Saurabh Kumar

Assistant Professor

Dr. Saurabh Kumar is an assistant professor at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad. Prior to joining IIT Hyderabad, he was a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Department of Computer Science, McCormick School of Engineering, Northwestern University, USA. He holds a PhD degree from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur. He has also served as an Assistant Programmer with the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), India. In CBI, his work revolved around Cybercrime and cyber forensics, along with managing the department's information technology-related activities. Dr. Saurabh's research interests include cybersecurity, Android security, malware analysis, and cyber forensics.
